Biomimicry

Material

Dimensions
H170 × W80 × D70 cm
Shown
All Solo!
The Miscellaneous of Being and Living

Biomimicry engages materials deemed without value. Using discarded plastic bags—transparent skins of consumption—Julia Hung fuses them through heat and pressure into organic forms that appear to breathe. Resisting hierarchies of worth, the work reflects on processes of transformation and questions how value is assigned and who determines what is expendable.

Aquachroma series

2021

Medium
Enamelled copper wire
Photos
Zhi Yang Lin

Commissioned for Ji-An Japanese Restaurant

A commissioned, site-specific triptych tracing states of striving, flow, and return. From left to right, the forms suggest ascent, sustained ease, and cyclical movement. Fish-like structures, built through repeated gestures in copper wire, move between effort and ease, individuality and connection—reflecting a continuous process of becoming in which paths unfold and converge.

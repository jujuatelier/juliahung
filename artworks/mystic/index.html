Mystic series

2024

Medium
Enamelled copper wire, Video Projection
Shown
Airy Disk
Photos
Chi Hung Chu

Through woven copper wire, projected light, and shadow, Mystic ⽞ (Xuan) explores unstable states between presence and absence. The work continuously appears and dissolves, allowing visibility and invisibility to emerge as interdependent conditions shaped through perception.

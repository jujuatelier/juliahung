Exhibition · 7 Nov 2026 – 31 Dec 2026

re.riddle Presents Wen 雯 — Julia Hung’s First U.S. Solo Exhibition

Handwoven copper-wire sculptures evoke clouds, currents, and shifting organic forms. Hung returns to her Chinese birth name, Wen, which evokes patterned clouds, giving sculptural form to a name she once resisted and now reclaims on her own terms.

Exhibition · 10 Sep 2026 – 17 Dec 2026

Secret Forms opens at Galerie Georges-Philippe & Nathalie Vallois, New York.

Presented in the group exhibition Secret Forms, Hung’s Wisps and Whispers imagines the night through delicate woven forms. Suspended like drifting mist, they evoke fleeting, surreal worlds that appear momentarily before dissolving into darkness.
